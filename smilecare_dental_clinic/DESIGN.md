## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical yet serene aesthetic tailored for a high-end, patient-first dental clinic. It moves deliberately away from the sterile, anxiety-inducing clinical tropes of traditional medical portals, choosing instead an atmosphere of modern wellness, effortless precision, and reassuring care.

The core visual style is a synthesis of **Clinical Minimalism** and **Tactile Soft-Glass layers**:
- High breathability with generous negative space to reduce cognitive fatigue and dental anxiety.
- Soft, organic contours featuring pill badges and generously radiused cards that feel safe, hygienic, and inviting.
- Crisp chromatic contrast anchored by oceanic teals and deep marine slate text, reinforcing authoritative precision and pristine cleanliness.

## Colors

The palette leverages restorative maritime teals, icy ambient tints, and deep slate typography to project clinical competence and relaxed luxury.

### Roles & Intent
- **Primary (`#168AAD`)**: Anchors primary calls to action, active navigation states, selected dates, and authoritative branding elements.
- **Secondary (`#34B3C7`)**: Accents high-engagement interactive elements, progress bars, floating appointment pills, and micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#0F617B`)**: Serves as deep interactive hover/active states for primary actions and focused clinical callouts.
- **Neutral / Text (`#183B4E`)**: Deep slate navy providing optimal contrast against pale backgrounds without the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas & Surface Tint (`#F7FCFE`)**: Luminous, airy ground that gives screens an immaculate, refreshing clarity.
- **Subtle Surface Overlays (`#E2F5F8`, `#D1EEF4`)**: Used for selected table rows, hover backgrounds, subtle card containers, badge fills, and segmented control wells.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`)**: Elevated cards, modals, and sheets requiring strong separation from the icy canvas.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its geometric foundations, humanist stroke terminals, and open apertures evoke optimism, technical rigor, and warmth.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Emphasize tight letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and assertive weights (600/700) to convey clinical confidence and clarity.
- **Body & Data**: Styled in medium and regular weights with generous line-heights (1.5–1.6) to guarantee legibility for medical records, treatment plans, and post-op care guides.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set in semi-bold with slightly tracked caps or title-case to maintain readability on luminous, small-scale chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop, a 6-column fluid grid on tablet, and a 4-column single-stream grid on mobile screens.

### Grid Breakpoints & Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px) margins, `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px) gutters. Cards and modal sheets take 100% width with vertical stacking.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 6 or 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px) margins, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px) gutters. Complex components like doctor profiles or appointment step-flows shift to dual-column blocks.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width bounded to 1280px or 1440px centered, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px) margins, `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px) gutters.

### Rationale for Spacing Rhythms
Generous spacing reinforces the calm atmosphere. Sections rely on `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` gaps, avoiding visual clutter and ensuring patient interactions (date picking, service selection) remain uncluttered.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Ambient Diffuse Shadows**, completely avoiding harsh, directional drops.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#F7FCFE`. Clean sky tint canvas supporting all upper elements.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers)**: `#FFFFFF`. Clinical cards, interactive appointments, and forms rest here.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Surfaces / Nav / Modals)**: `#FFFFFF` with 80% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

### Shadow Language
All shadows use the deep marine slate hue (`#183B4E`) at very low opacities with expansive blur radii to mimic natural clinic overhead lighting:
- **Subtle (Cards & Modules)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.02)`.
- **Elevated (Dropdowns, Hovered Cards, Selectors)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.03)`.
- **Overlay (Modals, Slide-over treatment panels)**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(24, 59, 78, 0.14)`.

### Surface Borders
Cards and containers use crisp, low-contrast borders (`1px solid rgba(22, 138, 173, 0.12)`) to provide clean structural integrity without heavy visual weight.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an approachable, friendly shape language with soft, pill-inspired radii that soothe clinical tension.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Feature `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) corners, delivering an inviting, polished container.
- **Nested Inner Modules & Inputs**: Use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) corners to preserve proportional nesting harmony.
- **Buttons, Status Badges, and Chips**: Use full pill shaping (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to communicate touch friendliness, interactive readiness, and modern healthcare elegance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `#168AAD` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `space-sm` vertical and `space-xl` horizontal padding. Hover shifts smoothly to `#0F617B` with subtle scale (`transform: scale(1.01)`).
- **Secondary Action**: Pill radius, `#E2F5F8` background, `#168AAD` text. Hover transitions to `#D1EEF4`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Pill radius, transparent background, `#183B4E` text with subtle border hover (`1px solid rgba(22, 138, 173, 0.2)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Fully rounded capsules using 12px labels (`label-sm`).
- **Upcoming / Confirmed**: Background `#E2F5F8`, text `#168AAD`, optional 6px pulsing dot in `#34B3C7`.
- **Clinical Category**: Delicate 1px border (`#D1EEF4`) on `#FFFFFF` with `#183B4E` copy.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Structure**: `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-xl` (24px) boundary, 1px border (`rgba(22, 138, 173, 0.1)`), padding `space-xl`.
- **Interactive State**: Hover elevates with subtle shadow, top border transitions to a vibrant gradient stroke using `#168AAD` and `#34B3C7`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Wells**: `rounded-lg` (16px), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D1EEF4`. Height set to 48px or 52px for generous touch targets.
- **Focus State**: Border shifts to `#168AAD` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(52, 179, 199, 0.15)`).
- **Placeholder**: Subdued slate `#183B4E` at 40% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons**: Dual concentric circles with smooth CSS transitions. Unselected: `#D1EEF4` ring; Selected: `#168AAD` ring with an inner `#34B3C7` solid core.
- **Checkboxes**: `rounded-md` (8px), `#168AAD` filled state with an immaculate crisp white checkmark SVG.

### Specialized Clinic Components
- **Dentition Chart / Tooth Selector**: Stylized vector tooth map surrounded by `rounded-xl` containers, highlighting selected teeth in `#34B3C7` with `#E2F5F8` wash highlights.
- **Doctor Credential Card**: Split layout with rounded portrait container, pill credential tags (e.g., "Orthodontics", "Implantology"), and instant appointment trigger button.
